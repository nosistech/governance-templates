# Using AI at Keystone Advisory: one-page guide (fictional example)

> This page is a summary. The full rules are in the Keystone Advisory AI Acceptable-Use Policy.

## Do
1. **Check the client AI choice** before you use AI on any client data. It is in the engagement folder.
2. **Use approved tools only**, with your Keystone account.
3. **Use one workspace for each client** in each AI tool.
4. **Open every source** in a deliverable. Delete any source that you cannot find.
5. **Get a second consultant** to review each deliverable before it goes to the client.
6. **Announce the notetaker** at the start of every meeting.
7. **Call back** on a known number before you act on an urgent request for money, passwords, or data.

## Do not
1. **Do not use personal or free AI accounts** for Keystone work.
2. **Do not use AI on client data** if the client said no.
3. **Do not mix two clients' data** in one chat or project.
4. **Do not put passwords or other secrets** into any AI tool.
5. **Do not bill clients** for time that you spend learning AI tools.

## Data tiers

| Tier | Examples | Where it can go |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Public** | Published reports, public filings, news | Any approved tool |
| 🟡 **Internal** | Our proposals, templates, methods | Approved business tools |
| 🔴 **Restricted** | All client data, personal data, secrets | Client data: approved tools, within the client AI choice. Secrets: never |

**Not sure? Treat it as 🔴 Restricted.**
