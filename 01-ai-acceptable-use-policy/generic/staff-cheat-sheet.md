# Using AI at [Company Name]: one-page guide

> This page is a summary. The full rules are in the AI Acceptable-Use Policy: [link].

## Do
1. **Use approved tools only**, with your company account. The list is here: [link to AI Tool Register].
2. **Check the data tier** before you paste or upload anything (table below).
3. **Check every fact, number, name, and source** that AI gives you. AI can invent them.
4. **Tell customers** when they talk to an AI system, and tell them how to reach a person.
5. **Ask first** if you are not sure: [contact].
6. **Report mistakes fast** to [contact]. We do not punish good-faith reports.

## Do not
1. **Do not use personal or free AI accounts** for company work.
2. **Do not put Restricted data** into a tool that is not approved for it.
3. **Do not put passwords, API keys, or other secrets** into any AI tool.
4. **Do not let AI make the final decision** about a person.
5. **Do not record or transcribe a meeting** without the consent of everyone in it.
6. **Do not install AI browser extensions or connect AI to company systems** without approval.

## Data tiers

| Tier | Examples | Where it can go |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Public** | Our website, published reports | Any approved tool |
| 🟡 **Internal** | Emails, drafts, meeting notes, plans | Approved tools with a business contract |
| 🔴 **Restricted** | Personal data, [client confidential information], financial data, secrets | Only tools approved for Restricted data. Secrets: never |

**Not sure about the tier? Treat it as 🔴 Restricted.**

## Warning signs
- An "urgent" voice or video message from a manager that asks for money or passwords. It can be a deepfake. Call the person back on a number that you know.
- An AI tool that suddenly asks for more access, or acts without your instruction. Stop it and report it.
