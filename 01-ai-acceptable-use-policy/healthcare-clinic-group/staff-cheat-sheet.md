# Using AI at Riverbend Family Health: one-page guide (fictional example)

> This page is a summary. The full rules are in the Riverbend AI Acceptable-Use Policy on the staff intranet.

## Do
1. **Use approved tools only**, with your Riverbend account. The list is in the AI Tool Register.
2. **Ask the patient before you start the AI scribe.** Record the answer in the chart. In California clinics, ask everyone in the room.
3. **Read every AI-drafted note before you sign it.** Check medications, doses, allergies, diagnoses, and plans. Delete anything that the patient did not say.
4. **Get a clinician to review** every AI-drafted portal message about clinical information.
5. **Use the minimum PHI** that the task needs.
6. **Report fast:** PHI in the wrong tool goes to the Privacy Officer. A wrong AI note goes to the patient-safety system.

## Do not
1. **Do not put PHI into ChatGPT, a free tool, or any tool without a BAA.** Not even to "remove the names later".
2. **Do not let AI diagnose, treat, or deny care.** A clinician decides.
3. **Do not sign a note that you did not read.**
4. **Do not record anyone without consent.**
5. **Do not install AI browser extensions.** They can read PHI on your screen.
6. **Do not put passwords or other secrets into any AI tool.**

## Data tiers

| Tier | Examples | Where it can go |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Public** | Website, patient-education handouts | Any approved tool |
| 🟡 **Internal** | Schedules, supply orders, emails without PHI | Approved business tools |
| 🔴 **Restricted** | All PHI, staff personal data, card data, secrets | PHI: only BAA tools approved for PHI. Secrets: never |

**Not sure? Treat it as 🔴 Restricted.**

## What to tell patients
- "We use AI tools to help with notes and messages. Your clinician reviews everything. You can say no to the AI scribe at any time."

**Questions:** Privacy Officer (compliance@riverbend.example) or Medical Director
