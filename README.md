# AI Governance Templates

Free, practical templates that help small and mid-size companies use AI safely. Each template comes ready to use, with worked examples for three industries.

By [NosisTech LLC](https://nosistech.com).

## What's inside

| # | Template | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [AI acceptable-use policy](01-ai-acceptable-use-policy/) | Sets the rules for how staff use AI tools at work | ✅ Available |
| 02 | AI tool inventory and risk register | Lists the AI tools that a company uses, with a risk score for each | Coming soon |
| 03 | AI vendor security questionnaire | Gives the questions to ask before you approve an AI vendor | Coming soon |
| 04 | AI incident-response playbook | Explains what to do when an AI tool causes harm or a data leak | Coming soon |

The four templates work together. The policy sets the rules. The register lists the approved tools. The questionnaire decides which tools get approved. The playbook handles problems.

## How to use a template

1. Choose the version that is closest to your company.
2. Download the Word file, or copy the Markdown file.
3. Replace every item in `[square brackets]`. Delete the parts that do not apply to you.
4. Ask your legal counsel to review the final document.
5. Give the one-page staff guide to everyone who uses AI tools.
6. Review the policy at least every 12 months.

## Built on public frameworks

Each template has an appendix that maps its sections to these frameworks:

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (AI 100-1)
- NIST Generative AI Profile (AI 600-1)
- ISO/IEC 42001:2023, Annex A areas
- EU AI Act
- OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2026

Alignment with a framework does not mean legal compliance.

## Important

These templates are not legal advice. Laws about AI change often and differ by state and country. The legal references are current as of October 2026. Ask your legal counsel before you adopt any template.
